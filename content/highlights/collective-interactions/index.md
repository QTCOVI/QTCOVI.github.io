> S. Sowlati-Hashjin, V. Šadek, S. Sadjadi, M. Karttunen, Á. Martín Pendás, C. Foroutan-Nejad,
> *Collective interactions among organometallics are exotic bonds hidden on lab shelves*,
> **Nature Communications** 13, 2069 (2022). [doi:10.1038/s41467-022-29504-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29504-0) · open access

## The problem

Every chemist learns to draw bonds as lines between pairs of atoms. The recent discovery of an unusual Na–B bond in
NaBH₃⁻ hinted that this pairwise picture can miss part of the story. Are such bonds rare curiosities, or have we
simply not been looking?

## What we did

In collaboration with Cina Foroutan-Nejad and colleagues, we used the Interacting Quantum
Atoms energy decomposition to examine more than 100 systems isoelectronic with NaBH₃⁻, of general formula
[M<sup>a+</sup>AR₃]<sup>b−</sup> (A = C, B, Al).

{{< figure src="geometry.jpg" caption="Collective bonding cannot be drawn as a Lewis structure. The direct 1,2 metal–central atom contact is weak or even destabilizing, while the 1,3 contacts with the substituents are strongly stabilizing. Sowlati-Hashjin et al., Nat. Commun. 2022, CC BY 4.0." >}}

We found that the metal often binds more efficiently to the *substituents* on the central atom than to the central
atom itself. To measure this we introduced the **exchange–correlation interaction collectivity index (ICI<sub>XC</sub>)**,
which runs from 1 for an ordinary pairwise bond to 0 for a fully collective interaction.

{{< figure src="vxc-vc.jpg" caption="Plotting the exchange–correlation against the Coulomb component of the IQA interaction energy maps the nature of each bond. Sowlati-Hashjin et al., Nat. Commun. 2022, CC BY 4.0." >}}

## Why it matters

- **Collective bonding is the rule, not the exception** among organometallics. Some organolithium and organomagnesium
  species, reagents found on the shelf of nearly every synthesis lab, show the lowest ICI<sub>XC</sub> values of all.
- **A new category in the hierarchy of chemical bonds**, next to two-centre two-electron bonds and multicentre bonds.
- **A practical, quantitative tool.** ICI<sub>XC</sub> can be computed for any molecule from an IQA analysis.
